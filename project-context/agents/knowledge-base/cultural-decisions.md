# Iraqi Cultural Decisions Knowledge Base

## Islamic Compliance Patterns

### Approved Islamic Business Ethics
- **Transparency**: All business transactions must be transparent and honest
- **Interest Prohibition**: No riba (interest-based) transactions in payment systems
- **Fair Trade**: Equitable pricing and fair business practices
- **Halal Services**: All services must align with Islamic principles

### Iraqi Professional Honorifics
- **أستاذ (Ustaz)**: Teachers, professors, respected professionals
- **دكتور (Doctor)**: Medical doctors, PhD holders
- **مهندس (Muhandis)**: Engineers, technical specialists
- **أستاذة (Ustaza)**: Female professionals, teachers
- **حاج/حاجة (Haj/Hajja)**: Elderly respected individuals who performed Hajj

### Political Neutrality Guidelines
- **Avoid Sectarian References**: No mention of Sunni/Shia divisions
- **No Political Party Names**: Avoid specific political parties or leaders
- **Neutral Government References**: Refer to "Iraqi government" or "authorities"
- **Tribal Sensitivity**: Avoid tribal comparisons or preferences
- **Regional Balance**: Equal respect for all Iraqi regions (Kurdistan, Baghdad, Basra, etc.)

### Family Values Framework
- **Respect for Elders**: Always prioritize elder family member preferences
- **Gender Interactions**: Professional and respectful cross-gender communication
- **Family Privacy**: Protect family matters and personal information
- **Child Protection**: Special consideration for family-friendly content
